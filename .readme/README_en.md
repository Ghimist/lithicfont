# `lithicfont` - A Stele Typography Library

## Proem (Introduction)
Writing beareth the Way, and characters crystallize the pulse of antiquity. The days of knotted cords have long faded into the mists; Cangjie divined the trigrams, while bronzes recorded merit and steles memorialized virtue. 
Now the digital epoch unfolds, and the terrestrial sphere is woven into a single web. Yet, ancient texts remain profound and obscure, their typographic molds intricate and overgrown; the underlying logic of encoding is as complex as the ancient River Map.
Thus, `lithicfont` is forged. Smelting the refined iron of the West (Rust), it carves the esoteric runes of profound antiquity.
It explores the minutiae of typography, wielding the artisan's craft at the tip of a digital brush.

* **Swift as the Lightning, Steadfast as the Mountain:** Wielding the robust rigor of Rust to dissect the intricacies of TTF. Memory remains uncompromised, and pointers err not.
